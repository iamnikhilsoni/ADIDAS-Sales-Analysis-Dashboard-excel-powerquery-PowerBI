# 📊 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Brief One Line Summary
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Adidas sales performance, profitability, and regional trends to drive strategic business growth.

## 🔍 Overview
This project focuses on a comprehensive analysis of Adidas sales data to empower decision-makers with data-driven insights. By visualizing key metrics across various dimensions—such as time, geography, and product categories—the dashboard fosters strategic growth and competitiveness in the dynamic sports and athletic industry.

## ❗ Problem Statement
Effective decision-making in a competitive market requires a clear understanding of sales dynamics. This project addresses the need for:
- Evaluating total sales and profitability over time.
- Examining product demand through sales volume analysis.
- Assessing pricing and margin strategies to improve overall profitability.
- Identifying high and low-potential geographical areas.

## 📂 Dataset
The analysis is based on comprehensive sales records including:
- **Product Details:** Categories such as Men's/Women's Street Footwear, Apparel, and Athletic Footwear.
- **Sales Metrics:** Total sales (360.33M), Operating Profit (129.81M), Units Sold (1M), and Price per Unit.
- **Geographic Data:** Region-wise (West, Northeast, South, Southeast, Midwest) and State-wise sales distribution.
- **Retailer Information:** Sales performance across key retailers like West Gear, Foot Locker, Sports Direct, Kohl's, Walmart, and Amazon.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies
- **Power BI:** For data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard creation.
- **Microsoft Excel:** For initial data handling and organization.
- **Power Query:** For ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes and data cleaning.

## ⚙️ Methods
- **Data Cleaning and Transformation:** Handling messy records to ensure accuracy in analysis.
- **KPI Development:** Creating measures for Total Sales, Operating Profit, Average Price per Unit, and Operating Margin.
- **Data Visualization:** Implementing specific chart types (Area Charts, Filled Maps, Donut Charts, and Bar Charts) as per business requirements.
- **Time-Series Analysis:** Monthly distribution of sales to identify peak periods.

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Top Product Category:** Men's Street Footwear leads with significant revenue (81M).
- **Top Retailer:** West Gear is the leading retailer contributing 124M in sales.
- **Regional Dominance:** The West region accounts for the largest share of sales (37.73%).
- **Peak Performance:** The dashboard shows a significant peak in sales around April.
- **Sales Scale:** Total sales reached 360.33M with an operating profit of 129.81M.

## 🖥️ Dashboard / Model / Output
The dashboard features:
- **Dynamic KPIs:** Real-time summary of Total Sales, Profit, Units Sold, and Margins.
- **Total Sales by Month (Area Chart):** Identifies seasonal trends and peak sales months.
- **Total Sales by State (Filled Map):** Provides a geographical heat map of performance.
- **Total Sales by Region (Donut Chart):** Shows percentage contribution of different zones.
- **Sales by Product & Retailer (Bar Charts):** Compares performance across product lines and partners.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter data by Region and specific Date Intervals (e.g., 2020-2021).

## ▶️ How to Run this Project?
1. Download the Power BI (`.pbix`) file from this repository.
2. Open the file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the interactive filters on the right sidebar to drill down into specific regions or timeframes.

## ✅ Results & Conclusion
The Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a centralized view of business health. By identifying that the West region and Street Footwear are primary drivers, Adidas can optimize inventory and marketing spend in these areas while developing strategies to boost lower-performing segments.
